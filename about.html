Rooted locally, made easier online

Based in Goluwala, Hanumangarh, Rajasthan, Dhatterwal Suhag Bhandar combines the familiarity and personal service of a local shop with the convenience of online shopping. Our aim is simple: make it easy to discover useful, beautiful and dependable products, order with confidence, and get straightforward support when you need it.

Speciality in banglesEveryday, festive and suhag styles remain at the heart of our collection.
Useful varietyBeauty, personal care, lingerie, hair accessories, stationery and daily essentials in one place.
Personal supportOnline convenience backed by direct shop support for questions and order assistance.
