Privacy Policy

We use order information to fulfil purchases and provide support. We also measure website activity to improve the shop. This page describes the information and services involved.

What we collect

When you place an order: your name, phone number, delivery address, chosen payment method, and any promo code used. This is saved to our order records so we can fulfil and contact you about that order.

When you leave a product review: the name you type in, your star rating, and your comment. Verified reviews may appear immediately; unverified reviews are checked before they appear publicly on the product page.

On this browser: we remember your delivery details, an unresolved checkout attempt, and your most recent order slip so you can recover an interrupted order. Review submissions include a random browser identifier used for basic spam limits. Clearing browser storage removes these local records; check an unresolved order before clearing them.

Your cart: cart contents are saved in your browser. Product views, cart additions and checkout activity may also be sent as analytics events; placing an order sends its details to the shop.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. The website uses Google Analytics and first-party activity tracking. Google Analytics may use cookies and browser/device information. First-party analytics stores anonymous browser/session identifiers, product activity and campaign labels; it does not intentionally collect names, phone numbers, addresses or raw search text.

Where it's stored

Order and review records are stored using Google Sheets and Apps Script for shop operations. Cloudinary hosts uploaded photos. Order details may be sent to the shop through Telegram notifications, and support conversations may use WhatsApp. When shop staff use the optional AI assistant, selected product or order context may be sent to the configured AI provider. These services process information under their own policies.

Optional customer accounts / वैकल्पिक ग्राहक खाते

Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. If you save your details, your name, mobile number and addresses are stored in the shop's Google Sheets. Orders placed while signed in are linked to your account. Cart and recent browsing history are not synced to your account. You can delete saved profile details and addresses on your account page; Google sign-in identity and order records remain. For account closure or other data requests, contact the shop.

Your choices

You can clear saved browser data in your browser settings. This also removes your saved cart and checkout details; check any unresolved order first. Blocking analytics does not prevent ordering. Contact the shop for questions about access, correction or deletion of your order information.

You can ask us to see or delete the order/contact information we hold about you at any time — just message us on WhatsApp.

Questions

Contact us anytime via the Contact page.

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site changes.
